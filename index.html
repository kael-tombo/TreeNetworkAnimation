02 — Routing

Routes that decide themselves

Junctions weigh load, priority and health, then choose a branch. There is no single center everything must pass through — only neighbourhoods that know where to send things next.

Junction rules

Simple, auditable decisions at every fork: route, wait, fork again, or return.

Load-aware paths

Busy branches shed flow to quieter siblings without dropping a particle.

Quiet rerouting

When a pathway degrades, journeys bend around it — you notice the logs, not the outage.
